Wu'ayra Castle

قلعة الوعيرة
Hidden gem Free entry 45 min Moderate climb

The only Crusader castle in the Petra basin — a 12th-century outpost cut off by a rock-hewn ditch, reached by a single bridge. Most visitors drive past it on the road to Little Petra.

Why it's a gem. Roughly 40 visitors a day against Petra's 4,000. Best light is 16:30–17:30, when the ditch throws shadow across the gatehouse.
